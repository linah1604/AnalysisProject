A furniture warehouse receives daily customer orders that must be packed and prepared for shipment within predefined deadlines. 
Orders differ in terms of item type, quantity, and size, which affects their handling difficulty and required worker skills. 
To standardize the system, each item will be classified into one of three complexity levels (low, medium, or high) based on the handling difficulty and required workers. 
In addition, orders may require one or more specialized skills such as heavy-item handling, packing, quality inspection, or labelling.
Certain items, particularly heavy items, will require multiple workers working simultaneously to ensure efficient handling. 
The number of workers required for each order is predefined depending on the item’s difficulty and required skill.


The warehouse operates with a limited workforce distributed across daily shifts. Each worker has:
Certified skills (Heavy item lifting, Packing, quality inspection, and labeling before delivery)
Worktime and overtime (Must not exceed maximum worktime per worker per day and must consider worker availability)

The objective of the problem is to determine an optimal worker assignment and scheduling plan such that:
1. All orders are complete to be sent out on time
2. Orders requiring teamwork are assigned teams with the required skills
3. Worker availability constraints are satisfied
4. Completion time is minimized
